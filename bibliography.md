## Note on citation practice

Several sources are trade press or product documentation rather than peer
reviewed work. For a submitted paper these should be replaced where a
peer reviewed equivalent exists, and retained with explicit labelling where the
claim is about a commercial system's behaviour, for which vendor documentation
is the primary source. Product claims should be verified against the running
product before publication, since feature sets change without notice.
